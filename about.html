About RAASTAGO

RAASTAGO is built around one simple goal. We help you reach it better.

People travelling in Pakistani cities often do not know what is happening on their route before starting their journey. RAASTAGO brings traffic conditions, road incidents and community reports together so one question has a clear answer: which route should I take right now?

How the website is structured

RAASTAGO owns its own data — user accounts, road reports, saved routes and community confirmations. The website uses a mapping layer for roads and locations. Community reports and route-demo data are maintained by RAASTAGO.
